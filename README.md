## What This Does

Demonstrates that the dual tidal peak observed in Earth's oceans is a direct geometric
consequence of the Earth-Moon harmonic system, derivable from rational fractions alone.
No gravitational constant, no transcendental numbers, no fitting parameters.

Using the harmonic distance coordinate h = 181/3, the Metonic residual ε = 7/19, and
the lattice scalar 24 derived from the Law of Admissibility (R = 4), the calculation
produces exactly two tidal peaks per day as the integer part of a closed rational
expression. The observed daily tidal lag follows from the fractional remainder of the
same calculation.

The repository also includes a Synodic Coherence Envelope: an interactive visualization
of the full 29.53-day lunar cycle partitioned into four admissibility phase walls under
R = 4, confirming that the structural geometry governing tidal peaks extends coherently
across the complete synodic period.
