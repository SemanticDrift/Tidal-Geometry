## What This Does

Demonstrates that the dual tidal peak observed in Earth's oceans is a direct geometric consequence of the Earth-Moon harmonic system, derivable from rational fractions alone. No gravitational constant, no transcendental numbers, no fitting parameters.

Using the harmonic distance coordinate h = 181/3, the Metonic residual e = 7/19, and the lattice scalar 24 derived from the Law of Admissibility (R = 4), the calculation produces exactly two tidal peaks per day as the integer part of a closed rational expression. The observed daily tidal lag follows from the fractional remainder of the same calculation.

The same rational framework yields a discrete phase closure period. Solving the solar day alignment and lattice alignment conditions simultaneously produces N = 107,160,000 lunations (approximately 8.66 million years). At this horizon, the Earth-Moon system returns to exact phase closure with zero residual drift. This period matches the observed 8.5 to 9.0 million year stratigraphic resonance in deep time marine sediment records.

## The Model

The tidal structure emerges from three rational coordinates Earth-Moon framework:

- h = 181/3 — Harmonic distance coordinate
- e = 7/19 — Metonic residual
- L = 24 — Lattice scalar

The spatial frequency remainder 1/9 interacts with the lunar daily fraction 77/120 and the lattice scalar to produce the nodal distribution. Adding the Metonic correction yields the total daily wave peaks:

1778/855 = approx 2.0795

- Integer part: 2 tidal peaks
- Fractional remainder: approx 57-minute daily lag

**Phase Closure:**

From the solar day alignment condition, N must be a multiple of 94,000 lunations (approx 257.34 years). The lattice alignment condition requires an additional factor of 4 x 3 x 5 x 19 = 1,140.

N = 94,000 x 1,140 = 107,160,000 lunations

107,160,000 lunations x (19 years / 235 lunations) = 8,664,000 years
